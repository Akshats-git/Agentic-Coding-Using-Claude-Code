Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Data We Collect

To provide the Service, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, and password.
  • Expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Usage information, such as login timestamps and general activity within the app.
  • Technical information, such as browser type and device information, used for troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including displaying your expenses and summaries.
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • Improve the Service and understand how it is used.
  • Communicate with you about your account or important updates to the Service.

We do not use your expense data for advertising purposes, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in a database associated with the Service and are retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time through the support channels listed below.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (for example, hosting or font services) to operate the Service. These providers may process limited technical information, such as IP addresses, solely to deliver their services to us. We do not share your expense data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the support channels listed within the app.
